
This folder contains the output visualizations and screenshots generated during the execution of the Car Price Prediction with Machine Learning task.

The outputs demonstrate the dataset exploration, data analysis, model performance, and car price prediction results obtained from the trained Machine Learning model.

Output Visualizations
1. actual_vs_predicted.png

Displays the comparison between the actual car selling prices and the prices predicted by the Random Forest Regression model.

2. price_distribution.png

Shows the distribution of car selling prices in the dataset.

3. present_price_vs_selling_price.png

Displays the relationship between the present market price and the selling price of cars.

4. driven_kms_vs_selling_price.png

Shows the relationship between kilometers driven and the selling price of cars.
